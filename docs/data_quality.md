  **Decision:** Agent uses the unaccented address.
- **Instalment minimum.** §3 table: credit card 'parcela mínima de R$ 100,00' for all 12x; §3.1: 2–3x min R$50, 4–6x min R$80, 7–12x min R$100.  
  **Decision:** §3.1 is more specific and wins; implemented in `business.installment_options` (2–3x read as 'parcel must be ≥ R$50').
- **Defect window.** §4.2: exchange for manufacturing defect within 30 days, then go to the manufacturer; §8.1: 90-day legal warranty for manufacturing defects.  
  **Decision:** ≤30 days: store exchange; 31–90 days: the agent does not deny, explains the legal warranty and opens a ticket for the team.
- **Regret right scope.** §4.1 grants the 7-day right of regret to *online* purchases; orders data has no sales-channel column.  
  **Decision:** Agent asks whether the purchase was online when it matters; windows are computed from the delivery estimate.
- **'Região metropolitana'.** §5.1 gives local delivery rules for the 'metropolitan region of Campo Grande' without listing municipalities.  
  **Decision:** Only the city of Campo Grande is treated as local; other cities get Correios/Jadlog rules plus 'confirm with the team'.
- **Free shipping threshold.** §5.1 says free shipping 'acima de R$ 500,00'.  
  **Decision:** Strictly greater than R$ 500.00 (exactly R$ 500.00 pays the R$ 35.00 fee).
